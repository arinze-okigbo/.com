Campus Bookshelf is a student-focused marketplace for buying, selling, and exchanging books within a campus community. It is designed to make academic materials more affordable, accessible, and easier to circulate among students.

## Overview
The project explores how a simple digital marketplace can reduce textbook costs and improve peer-to-peer resource sharing on campus. Instead of relying only on external vendors, students can connect directly with one another to exchange books they no longer need.

## Features
- Student-to-student book listings
- Local campus-focused discovery
- Buying, selling, and exchange workflows
- Affordable access to academic materials

## Why This Project Matters
College students often face high costs for books and course materials. Campus Bookshelf was built as a practical solution to help students save money, reuse resources, and strengthen community-based exchange.
