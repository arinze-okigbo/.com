I'm transferring to New York University as a Computer Science major, Class of 2028. Trinity College-Hartford gave me more than I can fit in a post, mentors who saw what I was trying to build, teammates on the track, and the room to start Splita. But more than anything, it gave me people: friends who became family, classmates who pushed me, and a community I'll carry with me long after I leave Hartford. Huge thanks to Ewa Syta for leading a CS department that pushed my thinking, especially around security and identity, to Coach John Michael Mason for the support on and off the track, and to Danny Briere and Richard Guha for the guidance that shaped how I build. To my Splita co-founders Will Clarkson and Dayo Adebari, this move is for the company too. NYU and NYC mean proximity: to the startup ecosystem we're building in, to the people pushing fintech and security forward, and to a CS program that matches where I'm headed. Excited to keep building Splita and meet the people doing interesting work here. If you're in NYC, let's connect. #NYU #ComputerScience #Splita #Fintech
